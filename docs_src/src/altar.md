# The Altar of Hope

The Altar of Hope is the meta-progression shrine: between runs you spend Candles of Hope on permanent unlocks. All of its regions read - the Living City, the hero shrines and their memory tracks, the trinket and item collections, the general upgrades, and the cosmetics.

A few notes:

- Purchases that the game gates behind a hold-to-confirm work with a plain Enter through the mod; no key-holding dexterity checks.
- Hero tracks read each step with its reward and cost, and locked steps say what unlocking them takes.
- Memory slots and their choices read like any other list, and a hero's collected memories show up on their sheet's conditions tab afterwards.
- The collection screens read each item with its full text in the buffer, including the ones you have not earned yet, so you can decide what to want.

Candles are earned by suffering (a run), and spent here. The exchange rate on hope is about what the franchise's tone would lead you to expect.
