# Credits

Thank you to Chaosbringer216 and his wonderful work on [Say the Spire 2](https://github.com/bradjrenshaw/say-the-spire2). The buffer model, the glance philosophy, and the controller layout were either copied or blatantly lifted (with his permission), and the mod would be a much clunkier product without that head start.

Speech goes through [Prism](https://github.com/ethindp/prism/), which has alleviated much of the screen reader headaches.

The mod loads through [BepInEx](https://github.com/BepInEx/BepInEx), and the installer is adapted from the [Non-Visual Calculus](https://github.com/rashadnaqeeb/NonVisualCalculus) installer.

Finally, thank you for playing and the bug reports; the mod would be worse off without your feedback.
